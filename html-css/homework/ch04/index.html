Why do I enjoy Billiards

I recently have started playing with friends and have found that it's relaxing in a way. It is also a good way to stay off my phone. I enjoy the process of getting a little better every time.

My tips

My tips to slowly get better is to practice one thing at a time if it's a more complicated technique. Play often if you can and pick up some tricks from people better than you.
